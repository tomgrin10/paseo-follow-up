# Follow-up

**Every agent, one inbox.**

Your agents work in parallel. Your attention doesn't. Agent tools are organised around chats: you visit each one to
find out whether it needs you. Follow-up is a Paseo plugin organised around your attention instead. The work comes to
you, in one place, ranked.

## What it does

- **Every workspace in one place.** All your workspaces, across every project, on one screen, each with its state, a
  one-line summary, and the issues, threads and docs it's about.
- **One inbox of what needs you, ranked by AI.** Questions, failed checks, reviews and merges from every workspace in
  one list. Agents ask for decisions as cards with lettered options and a recommendation, so you answer in seconds.
- **Automatic follow-ups, through merge, deploy and after.** Failed checks, conflicts and review comments go back to
  the agent that made the PR. After the merge, its tasks come due when the PR deploys or on a set day.
- **Dispatch starts new work** from a Linear issue, a link or a sentence: Follow-up makes the workspace, starts the
  agent and tracks it.
- **Compass ranks by what matters to your team.** Pick the people and sources whose words set your priorities, and
  the morning brief shows what moved.
- **An Overseer that knows all of it.** Ask one agent with every workspace's context anything from Home's search box.
  It answers, or passes your words to the agent doing the work.
- **Catch up** after a meeting or a morning away, one card at a time, on the desktop or your phone.
- **PR statistics** show who opens, merges and reviews PRs, and how long PRs wait for a first review.
- **Any provider:** agents on Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Copilot, Cursor and more get every Follow-up feature.

It never merges, requests reviewers, archives, answers a question for you or wakes a resting agent on its own.

## Requirements

You need Paseo 0.15 or later and Node 22+ on the machine Paseo runs on. A signed-in GitHub CLI there is optional and
turns on the PR features. Then open Follow-up in Paseo's sidebar: a two-minute setup walks you through the rest.

It runs on your machine with your own sign-ins.
[What it reads and writes](https://github.com/tomgrin10/paseo-follow-up/blob/main/docs/data-access.md).
